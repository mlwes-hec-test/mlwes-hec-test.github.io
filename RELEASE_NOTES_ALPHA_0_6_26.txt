Healthy Eating Companion — Founder Trial Alpha 0.6.26

Focused search and serving polish from Alpha 0.6.25 iPhone testing.

CHANGES
- KFC Australia / McDonald’s Australia heading is now actionable: tap it to browse a grouped full menu.
- McDonald’s discovery now includes fries, Chicken McNuggets and common soft-drink choices in the menu guide.
- Chain menu choices with no trusted nutrition remain blocked from Diary entry rather than being recorded as 0 Cal.
- Serving lists now defensively remove category-incompatible measures (including the stray Egg measure seen while reviewing Cheddar Cheese).
- Duplicate serving labels are suppressed when two internal keys describe the same practical measure.
- Calories and kilojoules remain displayed together for food energy.

This is deliberately a small, foundation-focused build so testing can continue without mixing in unrelated cosmetic changes.
