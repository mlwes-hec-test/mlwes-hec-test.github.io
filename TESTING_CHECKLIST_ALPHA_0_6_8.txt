HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — ALPHA 0.6.9 TESTING CHECKLIST

Recommended approach: test naturally first. Only deliberately stress individual controls after the normal workflow feels comfortable.

BEFORE STARTING FRESH
[ ] Publish Alpha 0.6.9 to the SAME GitHub Pages address.
[ ] Confirm Alpha 0.6.9 appears in the app.
[ ] Optional: Settings → Data, Backup & Privacy → Download Backup.
[ ] Settings → Reset App for Testing.
[ ] Confirm the app returns to the Welcome/onboarding screen.

ONBOARDING / PROFILE
[ ] Choose Australia and confirm State Or Territory is a list, not a typing box.
[ ] Confirm all eight Australian states/territories are available.
[ ] Enter a Queensland postcode while Queensland is selected and continue successfully.
[ ] Deliberately enter an obviously mismatched postcode/state and confirm the app asks for correction.
[ ] Confirm the home time zone is sensible for the selected Australian state.
[ ] Confirm Sex Used For Energy Calculation has a short explanation.
[ ] Check that major section headings are easy to distinguish and use consistent Title Case.

COMPANIONS
[ ] Tap several companion cards.
[ ] Confirm each description opens immediately without scrolling below the full companion list.
[ ] Use Back To Companions without changing the selection.
[ ] Use Choose [Name] and confirm the chosen companion is retained.

RECOMMENDATIONS
[ ] Confirm Daily Fluids is visible without opening extra details.
[ ] Confirm Detailed Nutrition Goals is collapsed by default.
[ ] Expand/collapse Detailed Nutrition Goals.
[ ] Confirm the recommendation acceptance controls are easier to reach.

NORMAL / FASTING DAY TARGETS
[ ] Confirm a Normal Day uses the accepted profile energy recommendation.
[ ] Change to Flexible Fasting Day and confirm the fasting target appears.
[ ] Change back to Normal Day and confirm the normal recommendation returns.
[ ] Use a deliberate Different Energy Target for one date and confirm it does not silently replace the normal profile recommendation for other dates.

FOOD LIBRARY — DATABASE COVERAGE
[ ] Open All Resources with a blank search and confirm familiar foods are already visible.
[ ] Search “cappu” and confirm a cappuccino/coffee-with-milk result appears near the top.
[ ] Search “carrot cake” and confirm carrot-cake records appear rather than plain carrot.
[ ] Search common foods you personally use: bacon, ham, cheese, yoghurt, egg, bread, English muffin, sausage, steak, chicken, fish, potato, pumpkin, rice, pasta, cereal/oats, crackers/biscuits, milk, juice, soft drink, pizza, soup, baked beans, tuna and salmon.
[ ] Note which familiar foods still cannot be found so the next data pass can target them.

FOOD SEARCH ON IPHONE/IPAD
[ ] Start a new Add Food action and confirm the search box is blank.
[ ] Type a partial word while the keyboard is open.
[ ] Confirm useful live results remain visible/reachable above the keyboard without first scrolling down or dismissing the keyboard.
[ ] Switch between All Resources and Online Foods and confirm one tab does not leave stale results in the other.

DIARY
[ ] Add a food to a meal and confirm it counts immediately without a Planned/Eaten question.
[ ] Confirm there are no ordinary Planned/Eaten badges or “Ate As Planned” step.
[ ] Edit an amount and confirm totals change immediately.
[ ] Delete one food from its three-dot menu and test Undo.
[ ] Use a meal’s three-dot menu → Clear [Meal], confirm the warning, and test Undo.
[ ] Add a saved recipe/meal from a general library screen and confirm a meal must be chosen instead of silently defaulting to Breakfast or Dinner.
[ ] Use Suggest inside Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner and confirm accepted foods simply appear in the Diary.

VOICE OR TEXT LOG
[ ] Say “carrot cake”.
[ ] Confirm the review keeps the whole phrase and does not convert it to Baby Snacking Carrot.
[ ] From the general microphone/Voice Or Text Log, confirm Meal begins as Choose A Meal.
[ ] Confirm Add cannot complete until a meal is chosen.
[ ] Start voice/add-food entry from inside a specific meal and confirm that known meal may be preselected and remains editable.

WEIGHT CHECK-IN
[ ] Save a new weight/date.
[ ] Confirm spoken feedback is brief rather than reading the entire written message.
[ ] Confirm the Save button becomes Saved ✓ after saving.
[ ] Try to save again without changing anything and confirm no duplicate is created.
[ ] Enter a different weight for an already-recorded date and confirm the app asks before replacing it.
[ ] Add/edit an older historical weight and confirm a newer dated record remains the Current Weight.

PROGRESS HISTORY
[ ] Confirm Weight Check-Ins are plotted on the Weight Trend chart.
[ ] Confirm small weight changes are visible because the vertical scale is reasonably tight.
[ ] Confirm chart dates are readable Australian dates rather than YYYY-MM-DD.
[ ] Confirm the summary distinguishes Food Diary Days, Weight Check-Ins and Activities.

HYDRATION
[ ] Open Daily Progress and use Add A Drink.
[ ] Add Water and confirm the fluid total increases.
[ ] Add a Zero-Calorie Drink and confirm the fluid total increases.
[ ] Choose Milk, Juice, Tea Or Coffee or Soft Drink and confirm the app asks for the exact food/drink record rather than recording only an anonymous volume.
[ ] Confirm Daily Fluid Target remains visible and editable.

RESET / DATA
[ ] After testing, use Reset App for Testing only if you deliberately want to erase this browser/device’s trial profile and begin onboarding again.
[ ] Confirm another tester/device is not affected by this local reset.

NOT YET THE MAIN TEST TARGET
Barcode scanning, nutrition-panel OCR and meal-photo recognition remain prototype/device-dependent workflows in Alpha 0.6.9. The next development round is intended to concentrate on barcode and nutrition-panel capture after this stabilisation build is tested.
