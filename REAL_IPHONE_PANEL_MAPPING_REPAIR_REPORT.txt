Why the supplied patterns failed
- The exact strings "Servings per package: 10.5 (...)" and "Servings Per Pack:
  10" already parsed when isolated and confident. A weak neighbouring word could
  reject the whole OCR line. The independent metadata pass masked weak notes,
  but its count regex accepted only end-of-line or an opening parenthesis after
  the count; "10.5 ? ..." was rejected. Split-decimal checks also scanned notes.
- "Sening an" did not match the narrow serving-size label expression.
- Spatial heading discovery missed fused "per100", losing two-column geometry.
- The text parser filtered out unitless "1040", then rejected the remaining
  "697 kJ" because its energy-token count no longer matched two headings.
- "AsPrepared" and "as-prepared" were not recognised; "Serving | AsPrepared"
  on a separate heading line was not associated with the per-100 reference.
- Adding newly recovered count details to a matching metric serving previously
  made the baseline merge treat the whole serving column as incompatible.
These are reproduced source rejection paths. The original iPhone word boxes and
confidence values were not supplied, so their exact historical values are unknown.

Targeted repair
- Pack count validation now ends at the count token and its boundary, allowing
  unrelated trailing descriptions. Numeric fragments, ranges, signs and units
  still invalidate the count. Confidence masking distinguishes damaged numeric
  continuations from noisy prose; "10 .5" cannot become an accepted 10.
- A damaged serving label can recover an intact metric/count pair only near a
  preceding pack declaration and before a nearby nutrition heading. It requires
  one unique candidate with a similar serving token or masked label, and stops
  at nutrient/ingredient/direction regions. It uses the shared count-unit registry.
- Fused per100 headings work in spatial reconstruction. Ordered energy runs can
  preserve the explicitly unit-labelled first cell while leaving a unitless
  neighbour unknown. A lone value still cannot select one of two columns.
- AsPrepared, As Prepared, as-prepared and prepared are recognised in heading
  context. Wrapped "Serving | ..." headings are associated with the reference.
  Weak heading evidence is not promoted; unrelated prepared prose is ignored.
- Newly adding count details to an unchanged positive metric serving can retain
  existing unread nutrient cells. Conflicting known counts or changed bases still
  prevent inappropriate inheritance. Panel cells take precedence on acceptance.
- Raw extraction, baseline catalogue/private values and confirmed merged values
  remain separately available in captureEvidence. No catalogue record is mutated.
