HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.23

BUILD THEME
Universal Search Foundation. This build deliberately concentrates on replacing food-specific search patches with one data-driven search controller.

SEARCH FOUNDATION
- One active search controller owns normal Food Library search.
- Query parsing separates quantity/unit from food identity and normalises common plurals.
- Generic food searches resolve to a food concept and use reusable facet logic derived from Australian food records and taxonomy metadata.
- Food varieties/types are derived from available food records where possible rather than requiring one-off UI code.
- Brand/product searches preserve every typed brand/product word and progressively narrow real product records.
- Unknown brand prefixes such as Cheer or Westacre route to product search without requiring the brand to be hard-coded first.
- Strong generic database concepts not explicitly listed in the taxonomy can still create a dynamic guided pathway.
- Unrecognised foods fail safely rather than substituting an unrelated food.
- Guided questions expose Back navigation and are kept to short, relevant steps.
- Calories and kilojoules remain displayed together at food level.

VOICE/TEXT
- Voice/text food identity now uses the same parser and candidate hierarchy as normal food search.
- Quantity/plural cases such as “2 bananas” are separated from food identity before matching.

ENTRY INTEGRITY
- Fresh Food Library/search/scanner additions clear stale replacement state so repeated foods can remain independent diary instances.

DATA LIMITATION
No static founder-trial build can contain every current packaged product. Product search can only show product records currently loaded from HEC/local/online sources; barcode and nutrition-panel capture remain the fallback for missing products.
