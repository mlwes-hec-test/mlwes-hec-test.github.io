HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.2

FOCUSED PURPOSE
Alpha 0.6.2 implements the navigation, Daily Progress, Diary, Food Library, Meal Planner, hydration and food-group changes agreed during Mal's Alpha 0.6.1 review. The built-in food list deliberately remains a small trial sample. A larger staged food database is planned for Alpha 0.6.3 after this interface is tested.

DAILY PROGRESS
- Remains the first screen shown after setup.
- Uses one compact Home / title / help header.
- Uses one swipeable date card with Today, Tomorrow and Yesterday wording; tap the date for a calendar.
- Removes the large help panel, Open Today's Diary, quick-action tiles, daily note and separate Save Today's Update button.
- Displays compact Energy, Protein, Carbohydrate, Fat, Fibre, Sugars, Sodium, Hydration and Steps progress.
- Adds personalised Australian five-food-group targets: Veges & Legumes, Fruit, Grains, Protein Foods and Dairy & Alternatives.
- Adds a seven-day food-group average so fasting and unusual days have context.
- Renames Water to Hydration.
- Hydration includes logged drinks, estimated food moisture and additional drinks not already logged.
- Uses Australian age, sex and life-stage hydration starting references rather than the previous 30 mL per kg formula.
- Additional drinks and steps save automatically.
- Uses a compact Today's Summary that hides zero-value details.

HOME
- Centres the seven-room Companion Circle.
- Adds Shopping List as its own Circle room.
- Circle rooms: Daily Progress, Diary, Food Library, Meal Planner, Shopping List, Progress & Weight and Settings.
- Moves the daily joke, thought, fact, quote or tip beneath the Circle.
- Moves Reset Founder Trial into Settings.

DIARY & DAY PLAN
- Uses the same compact swipeable date control as Daily Progress.
- Day Settings displays Save Day Settings only after Day Type or Energy Target changes.
- Date changes with unsaved settings offer Save, Discard or Stay Here.
- Uses a compact, swipeable summary for Goal / Consumed / Remaining, macronutrients and food groups.
- Uses familiar meal sections with individual food rows, meal totals, plus buttons and three-dot action menus.
- Supports All, Eaten and Planned views.
- Keeps planned food separate until it is marked eaten.

MEAL PLANNER
- Allows one, several or all meal periods to be ticked for automatic planning.
- Generates one separate suggestion card per selected meal.
- Adds Accept Meal and individual Try Again actions.
- Adds Try Again for Selected Meals.
- Avoids immediately repeating a rejected suggestion during the planning session.
- Protects existing plans by offering Add Alongside Existing or Replace Existing Planned Items.
- Accepted suggestions are added as Planned, never automatically as Eaten.
- Suggestions include food-group contributions.

FOOD LIBRARY
- Uses a compact list-style Resources layout with search and tabs for All Resources, Saved Foods, My Foods, My Recipes and Saved Meals.
- Keeps careful typo matching and exact-match prioritisation.
- Shows a close-spelling explanation when appropriate.
- Provides clear Save and Review/Add actions.
- The floating plus menu opens Create Custom Food, Create Recipe and Scan & Review.

CALCULATION AND DATA SAFEGUARDS
- Food and recipe entries now carry food-group and estimated water contributions where the trial record contains them.
- Voice/text-reviewed entries and Meal Planner entries carry the same calculation metadata.
- Older diary entries calculate missing food-group and hydration values from their matching food record where possible.
- Custom foods without enough information remain unclassified rather than being guessed.

TESTING COMPLETED BEFORE RELEASE
- JavaScript syntax checks passed for app.js and alpha06.js.
- Browser interaction test passed with no runtime errors.
- Tested Daily Progress opening, official hydration target, five food groups, Home Circle, all seven Circle rooms, Diary dirty-save behaviour, date-change protection, multi-meal planning, Try Again, accepted planned meals, Food Library list and typo search.
