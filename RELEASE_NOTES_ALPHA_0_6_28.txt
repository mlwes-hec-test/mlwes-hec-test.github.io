HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.28
FOOD INTELLIGENCE, NATURAL SERVINGS & QUICK-LOG REFINEMENT

PURPOSE
Alpha 0.6.28 is a focused founder-test build based on the Alpha 0.6.27 Food
Intelligence architecture. It addresses the problems exposed during like-for-like
HEC / Diet Diary testing and the egg, bread and corn-chip search tests.

FOOD INTELLIGENCE
- Guided answers can now change the remaining question path rather than forcing
  every food through one fixed sequence.
- Egg is now branched by part:
  * Whole egg continues through relevant preparation and size questions.
  * Egg yolk skips cooking and goes to yolk/gram quantity choices.
  * Egg white skips cooking and supports egg-white count, tablespoon, mL and grams.
  * Whole-egg units are removed from separated yolk/white entry.
- Plain egg searches retain the ordinary Chicken assumption internally without
  requiring a separate species question in the main egg path.
- Added fat/oil is only retained in the whole-egg guided sequence when the chosen
  preparation makes it relevant (currently Fried).
- Back One Step remains able to change the branch; changing Yolk to White changes
  the remaining serving pathway.

BREAD
- Bread source wording is now Homemade vs Commercial / Bought rather than treating
  all commercial bread as packaged.
- Commercial / Bought bread now asks where it is from before showing records:
  Bakery / Café / Restaurant; Supermarket / Store Brand; Bread Brand; or
  Not Sure / Typical.
- Supermarket/store and bread-brand routes allow the user to type identifying
  information such as Woolworths, Coles, Tip Top or Sunblest and rerun search.
- Wrap/tortilla remains separate from ordinary Bread search behaviour.

CORN CHIPS
- Corn Chip is now a recognised guided food concept.
- Generic Corn Chip asks source when useful: Homemade, Commercial / Packaged,
  Takeaway / Restaurant, or Not Sure / Typical.
- Commercial Corn Chip proceeds to Plain / Salted vs Flavoured instead of dumping
  a raw product list first.
- Flavoured Corn Chips asks for a flavour or brand/product search rather than
  pretending a plain salted reference is nutritionally identical.
- A small handful (about 25 g) is available as a practical approximate measure,
  while grams/package serving remains available for greater precision.

NATURAL SERVING & QUANTITY ENTRY
HEC's rule is now explicit in the UI and serving resolver: HEC does the maths; the
user describes what they ate.
- Food-appropriate measures have been expanded, including:
  * cheese slices;
  * bread slices;
  * individual crispbreads;
  * sausage forms;
  * egg yolk count;
  * egg-white count / tablespoon / mL / grams;
  * package-derived bars, biscuits, crackers, slices, pieces, sachets and similar
    units when a usable package serving is available;
  * metric cup/spoon choices for foods with a reliable mL basis;
  * a clearly approximate Small Drizzle (about 5 mL) for applicable pourable foods
    such as oils, dressings and sauces when HEC has an mL conversion.
- Fraction buttons ¼, ½, ¾ and 1 are shown for suitable divisible foods such as
  bars, slices, bottles, cans, tubs and other supported serving units.
- A packaged 80 g bar can therefore be entered naturally as ½ bar or as grams.
- Technical labels are cleaned before display; common package/database wording
  such as Package Serve, duplicated 100 g Serving labels, excess decimal zeroes
  and Mpara no longer need to be interpreted by the user.
- Diary, Daily Progress, Recent Foods and meal-overview rows use natural quantity
  formatting and pluralisation where possible and show the metric equivalent when
  HEC has a known conversion (for example 2 Eggs (90 g), 5 Slices (100 g),
  or ½ Bar (40 g)). New entries snapshot that metric equivalent with the diary entry.

MULTI-FOOD BENCHMARK
- The benchmark phrase "Two eggs on toast" is now split into two food components.
  HEC retains the quantity 2 for the eggs, completes the egg entry, then continues
  to toasted bread rather than losing the second food.
- This is the first protected compound-food benchmark, not a claim that every
  possible multi-food sentence is already parsed in Alpha 0.6.28.

QUICK FOOD LOG
- Quick Food Log now presents the entry method first:
  Keyboard Entry / Voice Entry / Barcode Scanning / Nutrition Panel Reading.
- The date/meal destination card is hidden initially and is shown after the method
  is chosen when destination information is still required.
- Nothing may be saved silently to Breakfast; date and one of Breakfast, Lunch,
  Dinner, Snacks or Other must be confirmed before diary save.
- Keyboard and Voice continue into the same Food Intelligence search engine.

DIARY
- The redundant diary calculation strip is hidden when there is no missing-energy
  warning, leaving more room for actual meals.
- Guided entries retain more useful human-readable names such as Egg Yolk,
  Egg White and size/preparation descriptions for whole eggs.
- Quantity text is cleaned in the main diary, Daily Progress, Recent and meal
  overview surfaces.

SEARCH / STATE STABILITY CARRIED FORWARD
- Exact food intent remains primary over containing terms.
- Same-query product ordering remains stable unless the user explicitly refreshes.
- Guided paths must remain compatible with their final nutrition record; HEC stops
  instead of silently substituting contradictory nutrition.
- Source-specific records and physical package labels should be preferred when
  available and should not be represented as more precise than the source allows.

ENERGY RECOMMENDATION
- Alpha 0.6.28 does NOT change the Recommended/Faster energy-target formula. The
  current founder-test Faster target remains separate from the food-entry changes.

DATA CONTINUITY
The two Alpha 0.6 browser-storage identifiers remain unchanged:
- healthyEatingCompanionAlpha06
- healthyEatingCompanionAlpha06Functional
A backup is still recommended before deployment.

CACHE
Service-worker cache: healthy-eating-companion-alpha-0-6-28-v1

FOUNDER-TRIAL LIMITATIONS
- Food-aware measures are expanded but are only offered where HEC has a defensible
  conversion or an explicitly labelled practical estimate. Branded package foods do
  not receive an invented piece/slice/crispbread count merely because the product name
  suggests one; package count needs explicit source data. The long-term goal is broader
  food-specific spoon/cup/drizzle/portion support as trustworthy conversions are added.
- Packaged product accuracy still needs checking against the physical package.
- Barcode camera, nutrition-panel OCR, microphone permissions and service-worker
  update behaviour require real HTTPS testing on the intended iPhone/iPad site.
