HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — ALPHA 0.6.3 RELEASE NOTES

This build implements the focused amendments agreed during the Alpha 0.6.2 testing session.

HOME AND STARTUP
- Fixed the oval Home orbit and placed all seven rooms at equal angles.
- New users arrive at Home immediately after profile setup.
- Later app launches open Daily Progress.

DAILY PROGRESS
- Planned food is visible from the start of the day.
- Every nutrient and food-group card distinguishes Eaten and Planned.
- Summary shows eaten, planned, projected and remaining-after-plan energy.
- Fluids target is separated from estimated solid-food moisture.

MEAL PLANNER
- Morning Tea and Snacks wording.
- Meal calorie guides are calculated from the daily goal and existing diary entries.
- Accepted cards change to Added to Plan and cannot be accidentally added twice.
- Accepted plans appear in Daily Progress immediately.

FOOD DATA AND SCANNING
- Explicit online search of Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central.
- Official AFCD search link.
- Barcode camera/photo/manual lookup.
- Nutrition-panel OCR with editable review fields.
- Guided egg and potato preparation choices.
- Meal photos remain review-first rather than producing unsafe calorie guesses.

SHOPPING LIST
- Edit quantity, category, spelling, brand and notes without deleting.
- Duplicate and typo prompts.
- Automatic category suggestions.
- Print, share, clear checked and speech entry.
