# Healthy Eating Companion — agreed screen blueprint

## 1. Daily Progress — opening screen
The app opens here after setup and on future visits. It answers: **How am I going today?**

Contains today’s date, energy and nutrient progress, water, steps, daily note and plain-English progress explanation. It does not contain the Companion Circle, Open Today’s Diary, Find a Food, Plan a Meal, Scan Centre or the long feature-card menu. A clearly labelled **Home** button opens the Companion Circle.

## 2. Home — Companion Circle
Home is a separate navigation page. It answers: **What would I like to do?**

The upper Healthy Eating Companion message presents one daily food thought, quote, food fact, planning tip or food joke. The selected Aussie Companion sits in the centre. The six Circle rooms are Daily Progress, Diary, Food Library, Meal Planner, Progress & Weight and Settings. No duplicate menu-card list appears below the Circle.

## 3. Diary
Contains the Food Diary & Day Plan, planned versus eaten entries, meal sections, Voice or Text Log, exercise and activity entry, water/steps links, daily notes, copy and move actions, confirmations and Undo. Opening Today’s Diary belongs here rather than on Daily Progress or Home.

## 4. Food Library
Contains Australian foods, packaged products, My Foods, Saved Foods, Saved Meals and Recipes. Search, barcode scanning, nutrition-label review, photo review, creating a custom food and food corrections belong here. Find a Food and Scan Centre are functions of this room rather than Daily Progress shortcuts.

## 5. Meal Planner
Contains normal-day and flexible fasting-day planning, meal suggestions, copying plans between dates, recurring plans and grocery-list preparation. Plan a Meal belongs here rather than on Daily Progress.

## 6. Progress & Weight
A sub-room provides Progress History, Weight Check-in and Printable Reports. It contains trends, energy and nutrient history, weight history and goal progress, without streaks, game badges or leaderboards.

## 7. Settings
Contains personal details, health profile, one current weight goal, language and region, units, exercise-credit choice, Companion and theme, integrations, privacy, backup, Household & Connections and optional Food Preferences.

## 8. Food Preferences — optional later setup
Not part of initial onboarding. Users can record Foods I love, Foods I like, Foods I prefer not to eat, Foods to never suggest and preparation/context notes. Future versions should also learn gradually from simple meal ratings. Allergies, intolerances, medical restrictions and cultural requirements remain in initial health setup because they are safety-critical.

## 9. Initial setup
Collect only information needed to create a safe starting plan: name, account details for the trial, Companion choice, date of birth, calculation sex or manual option, height, weight, goal, fasting-tool interest, safety-critical dietary information, normal-day activity and exercise-credit choice. Activity is worded **How active is your normal day?** with examples and instructions not to include separately logged exercise.

## 10. Navigation rule
Daily Progress is the default operational screen. Home is the central navigation screen. Every main area has a reliable route back to Home, and the Circle has a reliable route back to Daily Progress. Functions are not duplicated across several permanent menus.
