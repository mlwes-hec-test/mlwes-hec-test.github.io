HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.20

PRIMARY PURPOSE
This build fixes the recurring search-architecture fault that allowed raw AFCD/database rows to replace the guided food workflow.

SEARCH — PROTECTED UNIVERSAL GUIDED PATH
- Every ordinary food search now presents the guided path first.
- A broad “Pie” search deliberately presents “Pie, Curry” first and “Pie” second.
- Choosing “Pie, Curry” carries Curry forward and does not ask the user to choose Curry again.
- Pie continues through filling/protein and source (homemade, bakery/fresh, commercial/ready-to-eat, frozen, takeaway/restaurant) before quantity/unit review.
- The final food-entry screen uses a friendly guided name while the closest Australian nutrition record remains behind the scenes as the data source.
- Raw database rows are hidden behind a deliberate “Browse database matches” control so they cannot become the primary workflow after AFCD loading or online refresh.
- The guided surface is re-applied after local AFCD loading and online-result refreshes.
- Existing guided families remain available for sausage, egg, bread, potato, rice, pasta/noodles, meat, fish/seafood, dairy, cereal, coffee/hot drinks, burgers and sandwiches/wraps. Unknown food terms still enter a generic guided route rather than becoming an unstructured result dump.
- Burger King continues to redirect toward Hungry Jack’s in the Australian workflow.

WEIGHT GRAPH
- The Alpha 0.6.19 range controls are retained: 7 Days, 14 Days, 30 Days, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year and All.
