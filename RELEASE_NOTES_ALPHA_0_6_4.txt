HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — RELEASE NOTES ALPHA 0.6.4

DATA AND UPGRADES
- Reuses Alpha 0.6.3 storage keys so existing same-site browser data is retained and schema-upgraded.
- Adds Download Backup and Restore Backup.
- Adds Reset App for Testing.
- Adds Leave Healthy Eating Companion to remove local registration/profile and app data.

PROFILE, DATE AND TIME
- Adds optional surname.
- Adds optional country, state/province, postcode, home time zone and time-zone behaviour.
- Uses local calendar dates instead of UTC for Today.
- Daylight saving is handled by the selected IANA time zone.
- Detects device time-zone changes and can ask, follow the device, or retain home time.
- New records store local date, time zone and creation time where applicable.

HOME AND RECOMMENDATIONS
- Clearer Current Weight, Goal Weight and Daily Energy Target labels.
- Last recorded weight date shown.
- Calculation details collapsed behind help control.
- Fixes duplicate/inconsistent final calorie display.
- Adds tappable category-based companion content.

GOALS AND DAILY PAGES
- Fixes Maintain/Lose/Gain goal-change validation.
- Preserves decimal maintenance weights.
- Home entry to date-based rooms starts on Today.
- Contextual dated actions retain the relevant date.
- Adds Back and Home controls to applicable pages.

DIARY, DAILY PROGRESS AND MEAL PLANNER
- Planned foods are named and actionable in Daily Progress.
- Adds editable fluid target.
- Meal planner counts existing eaten and planned energy, provides full nutrient/food-group details, persistent View in Diary and Undo, and duplicate protection.

FOOD LIBRARY AND RECIPES
- Fixes tab/results mismatch caused by delayed online searches.
- Renames Favourite Foods and Foods I Created.
- Improves Cancel Adding context.
- Replaces persistent floating menu with closable Create or Scan actions.
- Adds guided creation choice for single foods, recipes and saved meals.
- Recipe builder supports ingredient search, custom/scanned ingredients, food-specific measures, live total/per-serving nutrition, retained drafts and Favourite recipes.
- Adds trial records/measures for English muffin, bacon, cheddar, sauce, margarine, sausage and steak.
- Applies clearer capitalisation to displayed recipe measures.

FEEDBACK, ANALYTICS AND FOUNDER TOOLS
- Adds structured feedback categories and Share/Copy/email handoff.
- Adds opt-in local anonymous feature-use counts.
- Adds PIN-protected local founder dashboard.
- Adds up to 10 locally managed invitation links.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- Founder invitations, insights and feedback are local prototypes and are not centrally synchronised.
- A secure backend is required for real accounts, central analytics, central feedback, secure invitations and cross-device deregistration.
- Online databases, barcode scanning and OCR still require broad real-device testing.
