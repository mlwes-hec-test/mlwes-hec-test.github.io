HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — FOUNDER TRIAL ALPHA 0.6.1

FOCUSED NAVIGATION AND PROFILE UPDATE

- Daily Progress is now the first working screen after setup and on later app openings.
- Daily Progress has a clear Home button.
- Home is now a dedicated Companion Circle page rather than a mixed dashboard and menu.
- Removed the duplicated Today panel, Open Today’s Diary button, quick-action row, separate inspiration card and long menu-card list from Home.
- Added Daily Progress, Diary, Food Library, Meal Planner, Progress & Weight and Settings to the Circle.
- Added a Progress & Weight sub-room for history, weight check-ins and printable reports.
- Moved the daily food thought, quote, tip, fact or joke into the Healthy Eating Companion message box.
- Food likes and dislikes are no longer requested during initial setup. They can be entered later under Settings > Food Preferences.
- Improved normal-day activity wording and examples.
- Removed future staged weight goals; the app now uses one current weight goal.
- Capitalised Morning, Afternoon and Evening in the home greeting.

DATA
- Alpha 0.6 local data is retained because the same storage keys are used.
