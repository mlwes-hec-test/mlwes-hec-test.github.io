Healthy Eating Companion — Founder Trial Alpha 0.6.15

FOCUS: AUSTRALIAN FOOD SEARCH + EVERYDAY DIARY POLISH

- Recognises Bunnings sausage/Bunnings snag/sausage sizzle, Chiko Roll, potato scallop/potato cake/potato fritter and more Australian terms.
- Voice/text logging shares the improved Australian search matching.
- Adds more food-appropriate serving choices such as serving, g, mL, slice, biscuit, piece, egg and sausage where sensible.
- Daily Progress now uses Grey, Yellow, Green, Orange and Red status bands and shows how far over/under target the user is.
- Adds Suggest Food actions for materially low nutrients such as fibre.
- Fresh app opening returns Daily Progress to Today.
- Recent defaults to the current meal and covers about the last 14 days, with quick meal filters.
- Tapping a Diary meal heading opens the whole meal; foods can be edited individually and swiped left to reveal Delete on touch devices.
- Plan Multiple Meals preserves the selected Diary date.
- Quick Log gains Cancel and only shows Stop Listening while needed.
- Weight graph uses more of the available width.
- Food source badges are more consistently visible.

Existing Alpha 0.6 local storage keys are retained so normal version upgrades do not intentionally erase tester data.
