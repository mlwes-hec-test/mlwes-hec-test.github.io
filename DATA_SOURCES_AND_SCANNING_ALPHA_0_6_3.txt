HEALTHY EATING COMPANION — ALPHA 0.6.3
FOOD DATA AND SCANNING NOTES

FOOD DATA LAYERS
1. Local Australian trial foods
   - Small curated set that works without internet.
   - Used to test Australian terminology, serving choices, five-food-group calculations and guided preparation.

2. Open Food Facts
   - Live packaged-product and barcode lookup.
   - Community supplied; records may be incomplete, duplicated or inaccurate.
   - Always compare nutrition values, serving sizes, ingredients and allergens with the package.

3. USDA FoodData Central
   - Supplementary generic and branded-food search.
   - International values may not match an Australian product or preparation.
   - Alpha 0.6.3 uses the public DEMO_KEY unless a tester enters their own API key in Food Data Sources.

4. Australian Food Composition Database (FSANZ)
   - Preferred official Australian generic-food reference.
   - Alpha 0.6.3 links to the official search rather than copying the complete dataset into the trial.
   - A later validated import should map AFCD foods to Australian serves, cooking variants and the five food groups.

SCANNING
- Barcode: live camera where supported, barcode photo fallback, or manual number entry. Product data comes from Open Food Facts and requires review.
- Nutrition panel: OCR attempts to read text and nutrition values from a photograph. Every field remains editable and nothing is saved automatically.
- Meal photo: image capture and review workflow only. The static founder build does not claim reliable automatic food recognition or calorie estimation.

PRIVACY AND DEPLOYMENT
- Local foods, diary data and cached online results remain in this browser's local storage.
- Online searches send the search words or barcode to the named external database.
- Camera access normally requires HTTPS and user permission.
- A production release should use a protected backend for database keys, caching, validation, moderation, rate limits and future AI meal-photo recognition.
